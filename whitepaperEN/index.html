01 — Introduction

What is OM?

OM is an ERC-20 token with a maximum supply of 1,000,000,000 (1 billion) tokens. There is no pre-mine, no team allocation, and no venture capital reserve — every single OM token enters circulation through user participation.

The OM ecosystem is built around a simple daily check-in mechanic. Users register, check in once every 23 hours, and receive OM tokens as mining rewards. However, OM introduces several game-theory layers that make strategic timing critical:

  • Halving Cycles — Mining rewards halve every 60 days, creating urgency for early participation.
  • Token Locking — Mined tokens are initially locked and cannot be transferred until the user pays to unlock.
  • The Burn Rule — If you don't unlock before a new epoch begins, your locked tokens are permanently destroyed.
  • Staking — Unlocked users can stake OM to earn additional yield.
  • Referral Rewards — Invite others and earn 10% bonus on their mining rewards.

This whitepaper is your complete guide to playing the OM game — from registration to advanced staking strategies.

How It Works

  1. Call the checkIn() function from your registered wallet.
  2. You receive the current epoch's reward (starts at 50 OM).
  3. A 23-hour cooldown begins — you cannot check in again until the cooldown expires.
  4. If your account is locked (not yet unlocked), the mined tokens are added to your locked balance.
  5. If your account is unlocked, your referrer receives a 10% bonus reward.
Tip

The 23-hour cooldown (not 24 hours) means your check-in window drifts earlier each day. Set a recurring reminder to maximize your mining output.

05 — Critical Rule

The Burn Rule

This is the most important mechanism in the OM ecosystem. Failing to understand this rule can result in the total loss of your mined tokens.

⚠ WARNING: Epoch Transition Burn

If you are still locked (have not paid 10 USDT to unlock) when a new epoch begins (every 60 days), ALL of your locked tokens will be permanently burned the next time you check in. They are destroyed on-chain and cannot be recovered.

This is irreversible. There is no grace period. There is no recovery mechanism.

How It Works Technically

When you call checkIn(), the contract checks:

  1. Have you checked in before? (hasCheckedIn = true)
  2. Is the current epoch higher than your last recorded epoch?
  3. Is your account still locked?

If all three conditions are true, the contract calls burn() on your entire locked balance and resets it to zero — before minting your new check-in reward.

Example Scenario

You mine 50 OM per day for 59 days during Epoch 0. You accumulate 2,950 OM in locked balance. On Day 61 (Epoch 1), you check in without unlocking first. Result: All 2,950 OM are burned. You then receive 25 OM (Epoch 1 reward) as new locked balance. Your 59 days of mining are erased.

How to Protect Yourself

!

Unlock Before the Epoch Ends

Pay 10 USDT to call payToUnlock() before the 60-day epoch transition. Once unlocked, your tokens are safe from burning permanently.

!

Monitor Epoch Timing

Use nextHalvingTime() to check when the next epoch begins. Plan your unlock well in advance.

07 — Token Release

Linear Release Schedule

After unlocking, your locked balance doesn't become fully available instantly. Instead, it follows a linear release over 10,000 periods, where each period is 23 hours.

available = balance − lockedBalance × (10000 − periodsPassed) ÷ 10000

Example

If you have 1,000 OM locked at the time of unlock:

  • After 1 period (23h): 0.1 OM available, 999.9 OM locked
  • After 100 periods (~96 days): 10 OM available, 990 OM locked
  • After 1,000 periods (~958 days): 100 OM available, 900 OM locked
  • After 5,000 periods (~4.8 years): 500 OM available
  • After 10,000 periods (~9.6 years): all 1,000 OM fully available
Note

Tokens mined after unlocking are NOT locked. They are immediately available. The linear release only applies to tokens that were in your locked balance at the moment you unlocked.

08 — Referral System

Invite & Earn

OM features a single-tier referral system. When someone you referred checks in and mines OM, you receive a 10% bonus — minted as new tokens, at no cost to the referred user.

Referral Rules

  • Referral rewards are only active when the referred user has unlocked their account.
  • If the referred user is still locked, no referral bonus is paid.
  • The bonus is 10% of the referred user's check-in reward (e.g., 5 OM if they earn 50 OM).
  • If the referrer is locked, the bonus is added to the referrer's locked balance.
  • Referral relationships are permanent and cannot be changed.
  • The system is single-tier only — you earn from your direct referrals, not from their referrals.
Strategy

To maximize referral income, encourage your referrals to unlock their accounts as early as possible. You only earn from their mining activity once they are unlocked.
